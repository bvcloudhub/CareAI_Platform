APPLE WATCH NOTE

Use Apple Watch as one signal source, not the entire clinical architecture

Care.AI can ingest health and mobility data that Apple exposes with the user's permission through an iPhone companion app. Apple's own Fall Detection can independently contact emergency services after a hard fall and about a minute of immobility. A production Care.AI design should not assume that Apple's emergency Fall Detection event itself is exposed as a real-time third-party API; instead, integrate permitted HealthKit/Apple Health data and, where appropriate, separately capture authorised motion signals in the companion app.
